FAQ

Quick answers

Can I run AI on my own Windows PC?

Yes. Free tools like Ollama run open models such as Llama, Qwen and Mistral on Windows 10 and 11. 16GB of RAM handles 7B to 8B models. A GPU with 12GB or more of VRAM makes replies much faster.

What is the cheapest upgrade for local AI?

For most PCs with 16GB of RAM, a 32GB RAM kit is the cheapest upgrade. For speed, a 12GB graphics card like the RTX 3060 12GB is the best value.

Do I need an expensive AI PC?

No. For chat and coding help with 8B to 14B models, a normal PC with 32GB of RAM or a 12GB GPU is enough. Big 70B models are where costs jump.
