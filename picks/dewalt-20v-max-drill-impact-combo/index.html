Use notes: which tool for which job

  • Drill/driver for holes and small screws. Use gear 1 (slide the shifter toward the chuck) for more torque and big bits, gear 2 for speed with small bits. The numbered clutch collar stops the screw from sinking too deep: start low and turn it up.
  • Impact driver for long screws and lag bolts. Deck screws, cabinet screws and lag bolts are its job. It only takes 1/4 inch hex shank bits, so use impact rated bits.
  • Stop the motor before you shift gears on the drill. DEWALT's manual says to let it stop first.
  • 1.3Ah packs are small and light. That keeps the tools easy to hold overhead, but for a long job keep one pack on the charger while you use the other.
  • Store batteries indoors. Heat and freezing cold are hard on lithium ion packs.

FAQ

Does the DEWALT DCK240C2 come with batteries and a charger?

Yes. DEWALT lists two 20V MAX 1.3Ah lithium ion battery packs, one 20V MAX charger and a contractor bag, along with the DCD771 drill/driver and the DCF885 impact driver.

Is the DCK240C2 20V or 18V?

Both numbers describe the same battery. DEWALT says the maximum initial voltage, measured with no load, is 20 volts and the nominal voltage is 18 volts.

What is the difference between the drill and the impact driver in this kit?

The DCD771 drill/driver has a 1/2 inch chuck, two speeds (0 to 450 and 0 to 1,500 RPM) and a clutch, so it is the tool for drilling holes and small screws. The DCF885 impact driver has a 1/4 inch hex chuck and is the tool for long screws and lag bolts.

How big a hole can the DCD771 drill?

DEWALT's DCD771 manual lists maximum recommended capacities of 1 inch in wood and 1/2 inch in steel.

What warranty does the DEWALT DCK240C2 have?

DEWALT lists a 3 year limited warranty, 1 year of free service and a 90 day satisfaction guarantee.

Can this impact driver change lug nuts?

It is not the right tool for that. A 1/4 inch impact driver is made for screws and small bolts. For lug nuts, use a 1/2 inch or 3/4 inch impact wrench and finish with a torque wrench.
