#1 seat gap filler. Stops phones and keys vanishing between console and seat on long routes.

Why this pick

The original Shark Tank seat gap filler stops phones, keys and change from falling between the seat and console. The 2-pack covers both front seats and includes an LED and slide pad. Some cars need a little trimming for a good fit.

Who it is for

Drivers who keep losing phones, keys, fries and change between the seat and the center console, especially gig and delivery drivers who are in and out of the car all day.

How to get the most out of it

  • Slide it in along the seat belt buckle so it moves with the seat.
  • Use the 2-pack for both front seats.
  • Push it down firmly so it fills the gap without blocking the seat controls.
  • The included LED and slide pad help you find small things in the dark.

What to check before you buy

  • Look at the gap in your car first, because some cars need a little trimming or fit tweaking.
  • Make sure it will not cover seat belt buckles or seat adjustment buttons.
  • If you mostly need a phone holder, a dash mount may be the better buy.
