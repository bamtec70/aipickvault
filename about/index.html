About AI Pick Vault

Who runs it

I'm Blake, and I'm based in Fort Worth, Texas. I've spent decades fixing people's computers, and this site is where I write down what I've learned in plain words.

What this site does

AI Pick Vault has plain-English guides to running AI on your own PC, so you can keep your data at home and skip the monthly bills. Start with the Local AI hub or AI for Beginners, or see if your computer is ready with Check My PC.

The site also has practical picks: gear for your home, your car, your tools and your desk.
