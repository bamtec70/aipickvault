What is Ollama? The AI models explained

Free, private AI on your own computer, explained without the jargon. Plus which model to use for which job.

Last checked October 2026. Written in plain words. No question is silly.

The short version: Ollama is a free program that lets you run AI on your own computer, privately. Think of it like a record player. Ollama is the player. The "models" are the records. You pick a record for the job you want done.

What is Ollama, in plain words?

Ollama is free software for Windows, Mac and Linux. You install it like any other program. Then you download an AI "model," which is the actual brain file. After that, everything runs on your computer.

  • Private: your words never leave your PC.
  • Free: no monthly bill.
  • Works offline: no internet needed once a model is downloaded.

Ollama has its own simple chat window, so you can type questions right away. Step by step setup is in our Windows install guide.

What is a "model"?

A model is the file that holds what the AI learned. Different companies make different models, and each has its own strengths. Some are better at writing, some at pictures, some at code.

What do the numbers mean? (size vs speed vs smarts)

You will see names like llama3.2:3b or qwen3:14b. The "b" stands for billion. It is the number of "parameters," which is a rough measure of how much the model learned.

What fits on my computer?

  • 8GB of memory: stick to small models: llama3.2:3b, gemma3:4b, phi4-mini, qwen3:4b.
  • 16GB: the 7B to 9B models run well: llama3.1:8b, qwen3:8b, mistral, deepseek-r1:8b.
  • 32GB: step up to 12B to 14B: gemma3:12b, qwen3:14b, phi4, deepseek-r1:14b.

Not sure how much memory you have? On Windows, press Ctrl + Shift + Esc, click Performance, then Memory. The number at the top right is your total.

Special helpers worth knowing about

  • "Thinking" models like DeepSeek-R1 show their reasoning before the answer. Good for puzzles and planning. Slower.
  • Vision models can look at a photo you give them. Handy for reading small print on a label or a letter.
  • Embedding models work behind the scenes so an app can search your own files. You do not chat with them.
  • One model per job is fine. Many people keep a small fast model for quick questions and a bigger one for careful work.

Your first 3 steps

  1. Download Ollama from ollama.com and install it.
  2. Open it and choose a small model like gemma3:4b or llama3.2:3b.
  3. Type a question in plain words. That is it.

Common questions

Is Ollama free?

Yes. Ollama is free software. The models in its library are free to download. You only need a computer to run them on.

Does Ollama send my chats to the internet?

When you use a model downloaded to your computer, the chat runs on your PC. Ollama also offers optional cloud models, which do go online, so pick a regular downloaded model if privacy matters.

Which Ollama model should a beginner start with?

On an 8GB computer, try llama3.2:3b or gemma3:4b. With 16GB, llama3.1:8b or qwen3:8b are good everyday helpers.

What does 8b or 14b mean in a model name?

It means 8 billion or 14 billion parameters, a rough measure of the model's size. Bigger models are usually smarter but slower and need more memory.
