Memory decides which models fit

For Ollama and other local AI tools, the amount of graphics memory (VRAM) matters more than anything else. If the whole model fits on the card, it runs on the GPU. If it does not fit, part of it runs from system RAM on the CPU, and replies slow down a lot. Here is how common Ollama models line up. Sizes are the default 4-bit downloads.

"Tight" means the model fits, but you have little room left for long chats and other apps using the GPU. The chat itself (the context) also takes memory. Speed: what the specs tell you

When a model fits on the card, how fast it writes depends mostly on memory bandwidth. NVIDIA lists 448GB/s for the RTX 5060 Ti. NVIDIA's current RTX 3060 page does not list a bandwidth number, but it shows a wider 192-bit bus with GDDR6 against the 5060 Ti's 128-bit bus with faster GDDR7. So for the same small model, expect the two cards to be closer than the generation gap suggests. The big difference is not speed on 8B models. It is that the 16GB card keeps 20B class models off the slow system RAM path.

For pictures and video (Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI and similar), 16GB also helps. On my 6GB card, SDXL came out corrupted and video workflows timed out. 12GB is a big step up from that, and 16GB is the practical sweet spot. The 5060 Ti's 5th generation Tensor Cores also support FP4, which newer image and video tools are starting to use.

Who should buy which

Buy the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB if

  • You want gpt-oss:20b or other models around 14GB to run fully on the GPU.
  • You want to make AI pictures or short video locally, not just chat.
  • You want a new card with a warranty and current driver support for years.
  • Your power supply is 600W or more (NVIDIA's number), or you are replacing it anyway.

Buy the RTX 3060 12GB if

  • Your budget is tight and 8B to 14B models are enough for you (they cover most everyday chat, writing and summaries).
  • Your power supply is around 550W and you do not want to replace it.
  • You find a clean used card from a seller that takes returns.

Skip both if

  • You need 30B or 70B models on one card. Look at a used 24GB RTX 3090 in our best budget GPU for Ollama guide, or at how much VRAM Llama 3.3 70B needs.
  • You have no desktop tower to put a card in. See the best mini PC for local AI instead.
  • Your PC only has 8GB of system RAM. Start with LFM3 on an old 8GB PC and a RAM upgrade first.

Before you buy: 5 checks

  1. Get the right memory size. Both cards come in 8GB versions. Look for "16GB" or "12GB" in the listing title.

FAQ

Is the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB better than the RTX 3060 12GB for AI?

Yes, for local AI it is the better card. It has 16GB of memory instead of 12GB, newer GDDR7 memory (NVIDIA lists 448GB/s), and 5th generation Tensor Cores. The extra 4GB lets you run 20B class models like gpt-oss:20b fully on the card, which a 12GB card cannot do.

Is 12GB of VRAM enough for Ollama?

For 8B to 14B models, yes. qwen3:14b is about 9.3GB and gemma3:12b is about 8.2GB in Ollama's default downloads, so both fit on a 12GB card with room for normal chat length. Models of about 14GB and up, like gpt-oss:20b, will not fit fully on 12GB and will spill into system RAM, which is much slower.

Can an RTX 5060 Ti 16GB run 30B or 70B models?

Not fully on the card. qwen3:30b is about 19GB and gemma3:27b about 17GB, so they spill into system RAM and run slower. A 70B model needs about 43GB at 4-bit. For those, look at a 24GB card or a big memory mini PC instead.

Should I buy the 8GB version of either card for AI?

No. Both cards are also sold with 8GB of memory. For local AI, memory is what decides which models fit, so only the RTX 5060 Ti 16GB and the RTX 3060 12GB make sense. Check the box and the listing title for 16GB or 12GB before you buy.

What power supply do I need for an RTX 5060 Ti 16GB?

NVIDIA lists 180W of graphics power and a 600W system power supply for the RTX 5060 Ti, with one PCIe 8-pin connector or a 300W PCIe Gen 5 cable. The RTX 3060 is 170W with a 550W recommendation. Check your power supply label before you order either card.
