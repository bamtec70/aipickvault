What actually matters in a local AI mini PC

  • Memory capacity decides which models fit. See our RAM and VRAM chart for Ollama.
  • Memory bandwidth decides how fast replies come out. A normal dual channel DDR5 mini PC is fine for 8B models; unified memory designs are much faster on big ones.
  • Upgradeable vs soldered RAM. Socketed RAM lets you start at 32GB and grow. Soldered means buy the size you will need on day one.
  • Noise and power, if it sits on your desk or runs all day as a home server.

Apple Mac mini (M6 or M5 Pro): quietest, easiest

  • Current lineup per Apple: M6 with 16GB, 24GB or 32GB unified memory (153GB/s to 170GB/s bandwidth)
  • M5 Pro with 24GB, 48GB or 64GB unified memory (307GB/s)
  • 5 x 5 x 2 inch body, Apple lists 5 dBA at idle

Why it works: Unified memory acts like VRAM, so a 32GB M6 handles 14B to 20B models smoothly and a 64GB M5 Pro handles 30B to 70B 4-bit models. Near silent and very low power.

Downsides: Memory is fixed at purchase and Apple charges a lot for upgrades. Base 16GB models are only good for 8B class models. No CUDA, so some Windows and NVIDIA focused tools will not run.

Skip it if: you need Windows, or you want the most memory per dollar.

FAQ

Can a mini PC run local AI?

Yes. Any modern mini PC with 16GB of RAM can run 7B to 8B models with Ollama. For 30B and larger models you want 64GB or more of fast unified memory, like the Ryzen AI Max+ 395 or Apple M-series chips.

How much RAM should a mini PC for Ollama have?

32GB is the sweet spot for 8B to 14B models with room to spare. 16GB works for 8B models. 64GB to 128GB is for 30B to 70B models.

Is the integrated GPU used by Ollama on a mini PC?

On Apple silicon, yes, fully. On AMD mini PCs it depends on driver and ROCm or Vulkan support for that chip; many 780M systems end up running on CPU. Check ollama ps to see.

Is a Mac mini good for Ollama?

Yes, it is one of the easiest setups. Get at least 24GB of unified memory, since memory cannot be upgraded later.
