Quick pick: For Ollama, VRAM is everything. A used or new RTX 3060 12GB is the cheapest card that fits 8B to 14B models well. A 16GB card (RTX 5060 Ti 16GB or RTX 4060 Ti 16GB) fits 14B to 20B models. A used RTX 3090 24GB is still the budget king for 30B class models.

Specs below are NVIDIA reference specs. Board partner cards vary slightly in clocks and size. We skip prices because they move daily, especially on used cards.

Why we lean NVIDIA here

Ollama supports NVIDIA (CUDA) and many AMD Radeon cards (ROCm), but NVIDIA cards are the least hassle on Windows and Linux and have the most community troubleshooting. If you already own a supported Radeon card, use it. If you are buying for AI, NVIDIA is the safer bet.

VRAM vs model size

  • 8GB: 7B to 8B models only, with modest context.
  • 12GB: 8B with long context, 14B (qwen3:14b is about 9.3GB).
  • 16GB: 14B comfortably, gpt-oss:20b (about 14GB).
  • 24GB: qwen3:30b and 32B models (about 19GB to 20GB).

Before you buy

  1. Check your power supply wattage and PCIe power connectors.
  2. Measure your case; many cards are over 300mm long.
  3. Prefer more VRAM over a newer chip at the same price for AI use.

FAQ

What is the best budget GPU for Ollama?

The RTX 3060 12GB is the cheapest card with enough VRAM for 8B to 14B models. If you can spend more, a 16GB RTX 5060 Ti or a used 24GB RTX 3090 runs bigger models.

Is 8GB of VRAM enough for Ollama?

For 7B to 8B models, yes. For 14B and up, the model will spill into system RAM and slow down.

Is the RTX 3090 still worth it for local AI?

Yes, if you have a 750W+ power supply and room in your case. Its 24GB of VRAM is the cheapest way to run 30B class models fully on GPU.

Does Ollama work with AMD GPUs?

Yes, Ollama supports many AMD Radeon cards through ROCm. NVIDIA cards are still the least hassle, especially on Windows.
